Professional profile

Target roles: IT Support / Help Desk Technician, Junior Network Technician, Junior Data Analyst.

I am Edward S. Vidal, a BSIT graduate of Datamex College of Saint Adeline in Parañaque City. From 2023 to 2026, I built a strong foundation in troubleshooting, networking, and IT support, and these are the areas where I am most confident.

What I enjoy most is finding the real cause of a problem. Whether a computer cannot connect to the internet, a network is running slowly, or a user is locked out of an account, I work through it step by step: gather the symptoms, test possible causes, apply a fix, and document what I learned.

I also use these skills at work. I am currently an IT Support and Network Support staff member at Atlas Shippers, where I help users solve technical problems and keep the network running reliably. This job taught me that good IT support is not only technical. It also takes patience, clear communication, and respect for other people's time.

My goal is to grow into a network and systems professional through certifications and hands-on practice. I value honesty, responsibility, and continuous learning, and I only claim skills I can demonstrate.
